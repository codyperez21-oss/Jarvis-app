Privacy Policy — Jarvis

Last updated: September 2026

Jarvis is a private, internal business assistant built for and used solely by Cody Perez (RVLTN Performance / Web of Abundance LLC) to manage scheduling, client records, and day-to-day operations. It is not a public application and does not have external users or accounts.

What data is accessed

Jarvis connects to Google Calendar (to read and manage appointments) solely on behalf of its single authorized user, for the purpose of scheduling and calendar management within the Jarvis application.

How data is used

Calendar data is used only to display, create, and update appointments within Jarvis. It is not shared with, sold to, or accessed by any third party.

Data storage

Client and scheduling data is stored in a private database accessible only to the application owner.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to info@rvltnperformance.com.
